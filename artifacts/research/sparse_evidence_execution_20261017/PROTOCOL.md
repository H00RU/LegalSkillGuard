## Intervention and exact information boundary

One same frozen8B call per whole original NDA, all17 original public hypotheses,
three original labels, unchanged original SPAN coordinates and full source.
For each hypothesis, explicitly decide a boolean for every index in the sorted
union of ACTUALLY available ordinary and acquired leads. Include no new index,
threshold, order-by-score, Gold, query identity, inferred role or legal advice.
Raw ordinary scores and every incremental scored record stay verbatim.

Output fields are evidence_flags, other_spans, choice, reason. other_spans permits
every original index outside the explicit check order; a flag controls each index
inside it. Thus no original citation is inaccessible. Decode by transposing true
bits and explicit outside indices only. It does not consult the choice, scores,
Gold, skill rules or annotations to add/delete a citation. Preserve both native
wire and distinctly labelled original-coordinate view; never replace native
content or create another native response ID.

This is a change of evidence decision policy and output representation, not a
pure field-order test. Unlike the old anyOf schema, the new schema does not
enforce relation/evidence coherence. Instructions still require it; observed
NM-with-citations or NN-without-citations remains uncorrected, scored and counted.
All arms receive the same new policy. No forced acceptance of a lead, citation
union repair, semantic judge, second model call, fallback or answer selection.
