# Fixed-library B1/B8 execution confirmation on the original TRAIN32

## Purpose and eligibility for this stage

The completed TRAIN8 output-partition screen passed its declared criteria:
the B3 bank improved native/full F1 by 10.44/10.54 percentage points, retained
both cohorts, and reduced wrong amounts from five to one. Empty-library
partitioning instead changed those scores by -5.17/-3.90 points. All 38 actual
requests, model-emitted values, mechanical merges and full-case scores passed
independent verification.

This stage checks the actual archived first-batch and final-batch libraries,
not another wording variant. It asks whether execution partitioning improves
the final bank and whether final-bank utility remains above initial-bank utility.
It is an existing-TRAIN diagnostic, not a new validation split, prospective
learning curve, official TEST score, or a new independently learned library.

## Fixed design

Use all the original 32 sources and their original 16-current/16-prior cohorts.
Use exactly the banks in the main run's publications/b1.json and
publications/b8.json, checking the latter against its terminal bank. Neither
bank is edited or combined with another bank. Both receive the same partitioned
execution tested in TRAIN8, with all original context preserved and no EC
forecast supplied. Every original public asset mention must be answered.

Compare four full-case conditions: actual B1 whole-case, actual B8 whole-case,
actual B1 partitioned, actual B8 partitioned. Verify the historical whole-case
requests against these exact banks before reuse. Report the original screen8
and remaining24 separately, in addition to all32 and current/prior means. No
source or answer is excluded by its score, and no interim score selects a bank.

There are 93 asset mentions, hence 186 logical partitioned requests. Exact
message/schema/generation fingerprints identify 17 already recorded TRAIN8
responses usable by B8; B1 shares none. Reuse those exact responses without
quality selection. The remaining 169 requests are generated once. Aliases
retain the true raw source path/hash and are not counted as new model calls.

No new teacher, EC, solver, citation filter, amount calculation, output repair,
fallback or quality retry. One invalid subanswer invalidates its whole case.
Merge only disjoint asset keys containing verbatim model citations and amounts.
Save all 186 partitioned predictions before grading against original case targets.

## Budget and decision

At most 176 new EV generations, expected169, plus17 exact-wire reuses; zero
teacher calls. Actor wall limit45 minutes; stop new dispatch at35 minutes to
drain the unchanged600-second transport timeout. Use the original healthy
four-worker Qwen3-8B and per-call sampling/context/8192-token output limits.
Report actual new-call cost separately from per-condition logical deployment
cost. Calls per case differ from whole-case execution and are not called equal.

The execution confirmation requires B8 improvements of at least2 percentage
points in both full-case F1 metrics, no loss in either current/prior mean, no
additional invalid case, fewer wrong amounts, and positive both-parseable net
gain. Separately report whether B8 exceeds B1 and how the B8-minus-B1 gap changes
under partitioning. A positive absolute score is not itself proof of stronger
continued skill evolution. Preserve all failed criteria and outcomes.

No prompt search follows a failed confirmation. A positive outcome permits an
adoption proposal only. Main-method integration, revised formal training and
paper-column replacement require the user's confirmation and a matched-control
plan. Original production code, training admission and v16 remain unchanged.
