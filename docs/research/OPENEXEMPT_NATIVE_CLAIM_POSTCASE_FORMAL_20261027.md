# Prospective empty-start formal version: claim feedback and task-conditioned skills

Prepared 27 October 2026 before the new historical-cohort placement answers.
The fixed adoption rule and one-shot handoff are in the task-conditioned TRAIN
protocol. This recipe is bound before that check; its manifest and actual build
are created only if the original TRAIN gate accepts. Preparation asserts no
benefit. Do not initialize this formal build with either diagnostic bank.

## Learning design

Use the same fixed original TRAIN128 source packet and original source order,
eight batches of16 sources, both EC and EV. Begin with an empty bank and empty
native-request cache; do not import earlier banks, responses, teachers or selected
checkpoints. Weights remain frozen Qwen3-8B. Keep the single captured four-sequence
service, original generation settings, original public asset/citation encodings,
direct answers and native grading. No validation split, outside legal solver,
fallback, new model or new paid service is introduced.

Before revealing each batch's labels, save every current prediction under the
evolving bank and same-case empty control; from B2 onward also evaluate the
declared frozen own-B1 control.
Own-B1 answers remain evaluation-only, never teacher or admission evidence.
Exact same-wire sharing is uniform, not quality-based answer selection.

The existing teacher sees the selected original TRAIN facts, complete targets,
actual parent/empty answers and mandatory bounded failure witnesses. Native
claim-aligned feedback distinguishes omissions, wrong amounts and extra claims;
system/schema deduplication is lossless. There is no added reflector model or
second teacher role. Keep at most two proposals per batch, the4096-token full-bank
cap, dependency-aware and complete-proposal assessment, adaptive balanced gain,
and all original current/prior native/full-output/negative/format admission gates.
Every proposal replays all seen TRAIN sources; no replay subset substitutes for
admission. Deletion, revision and merging remain available without forced growth.

The prospective execution change restores EC's original before-case presentation
and presents the same applicable EV skill text after the complete case material,
removing only the obsolete EV case-follows connector.
It does not add legal rules, per-case answers, a calculation interpreter or a new
task. This combined teacher-and-injection revision is NOT a single-factor ablation
of the earlier method. Original source epochs and all negative outcomes remain.

## Evidence and next evaluation

Monitor actual skill edits, projected delivery, bank tokens, later-batch utility
against own-B1 and empty, native/full-output F1, omissions, invalid and negative
claims, all raw origins and costs. Small changes between different incoming case
cohorts do not imply forgetting; same-case comparisons retain their role. Do not
promise monotonic curves or claim that more records alone establishes learning.

Freeze the unique actual B8 terminal bank before evaluation, without selecting
an intermediate bank using TEST. A separate prebound evaluation configuration
must match the actual inference/injection protocol for every condition. Reuse
historical predictions only when their complete messages, schema and generation
identity match; unchanged learned libraries need not be relearned merely to
generate new inference responses. Necessary matching mechanism controls remain
distinct from USER-DEFERRED extra independent main/ACE/Text learning repeats.
Previously inspected TEST is a development-informed fixed benchmark, not an
untouched confirmation set. Source-disjoint evaluation and the claim's scope
must be explicit; no TEST case content enters teaching or admission.

Preserve source/config/data and every terminal outcome on both disks. While
running, leave all frozen bindings unchanged; new scientific changes require
stopping/preserving and a new Run ID. Keep the original v0.23 manuscript and study
unaltered. Formal learning or a diagnostic gain alone does not complete the goal:
the final claim still requires downstream utility and credible matched evidence.
