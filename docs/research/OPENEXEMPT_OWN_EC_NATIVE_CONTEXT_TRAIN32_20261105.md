# Same-model EC context: native-reference encoding diagnostic

## Question and fixed comparison

The completed decoded-context diagnostic reduced missing EV claims (14 to 2)
but increased wrong values (18 to 40) and extras (9 to 22). Its native/full F1
changes were -0.55/-1.25 percentage points. It is not eligible for adoption.

That condition supplied long asset names and decoded Unicode statute citations,
whereas the original EV interface uses A001-style assets and citation_ref strings.
This final condition isolates that representation burden, not a new teacher,
additional legal information, gold classification, or a different task definition.

Use the same 32 released TRAIN sources, actual B3 bank, original EC predictions,
facts, statutes, prompts, original EV schema, sampling, seed, thinking and scoring.
Verify both public vocabularies are identical for EC and EV in every source.
Replace only the EC answer values in the previous extra-context message with
their actual native public_label_submission. Keep the same unverified-context
notice and message keys. No host-side citation filtering, claim insertion,
amount calculation, fallback, quality retry or prediction editing.

## Budget and stop rule

Zero teacher and zero new EC calls; at most 32 EV generations and 30 minutes
actor wall time, with dispatch stopped after 20 minutes and the same 600-second
transport drain bound as the decoded condition. Existing healthy four-worker
Qwen3-8B service only. Save all predictions before scoring the fixed cohort.

Report all 32 paired outcomes against the original parent and the decoded-context
condition, including current/prior means, both-parseable contribution, missing
claims, wrong amounts, extras and invalid outputs. Do not select cases or seeds.
The original cohort admission remains a diagnostic, not a new paper result.

If native encoding does not yield a substantive, interpretable improvement with
the existing retention requirements, close this EC-context branch. Do not keep
searching prompt variants. Even a positive result does not authorize production
adoption, revised formal training, or replacing manuscript numbers: those require
the user's confirmation. The v16 study and original artifacts remain unchanged.
