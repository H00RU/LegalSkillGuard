ordinary candidates into a provisional read list. No new planner/router/judge.

only source_queries. No legal relation, general inspection text, demonstration,
case ID/index/party/date/answer table or directive to emit a label is allowed.

mechanical neighbour/list-introduction operations; no learned semantic router.

provenance. The host never judges legal relevance or maps them to E/C/NM.
