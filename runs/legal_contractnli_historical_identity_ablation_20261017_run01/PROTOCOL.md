an independent learning repeat, an official TEST, or a score-selected bank.

Use exactly the same preselected B6 eight and B7 eight, and the actual bank
published BEFORE each stage in contextual Run03. Do not resample sources,

Two new arms per stage/source, 32 maximum NEW8B calls and zero NEW teachers:

1. `opaque_refs`: preserve every full past source byte, selected hypothesis,
   original relation and original evidence member, and all procedure/scopes.
   Replace past integer span markers by qualified alphabetic fragment keys,
   and the historical annotated `spans` array by `past_fragment_keys` strings.
   Every source/annotation roundtrips exactly. Current original source,
   integer SPAN namespace, all17 hypotheses, candidates and four native answer
   fields stay unchanged. Only the matching historical-reference explanation
   in the common system message changes. This tests the typed-identity
   rendering intervention, not a guaranteed behavioral invariance or the
   exclusive psychological cause of previous errors.
2. `procedures_only`: preserve all published procedure text, scopes and IDs;
   remove the full past demonstrations and their reference lists. No dangling
   references or invented examples. Keep the original system/schema unchanged.
