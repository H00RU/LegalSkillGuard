# Figure 2

Continued evolution improves subsequent-case performance beyond the first learned library. Each point is the mean over 16 new source cases, whose EC and EV first answers are saved before the current targets are released. Panels show native and complete-output F1 for the evolving, empty and fixed first-batch libraries. The fixed B1 library becomes available at B2; B1 starts empty. All eight observed batches are shown without smoothing. Post-label replay scores are excluded.
