## Abstract / 摘要

Reusable skills provide an external learning interface for language models whose parameters remain fixed. In legal rule application, useful experience must specify both when a rule applies and how its consequences are computed. We study batchwise skill evolution for a frozen 8B language model using exemption classification and valuation tasks from OpenExempt. Skills encode factual conditions, applicable provisions, exceptions, and necessary numerical reasoning as concise procedural text. The model directly predicts asset-specific citations and amounts through the same answer interface as the empty-library control. After a batch has been answered, a teacher receives released training feedback and proposes library edits. Publicly co-visible legal contexts determine which edits must be assessed together, and accepted components form one library after replay on seen training sources. The evaluation distinguishes subsequent-case performance from training replay and compares learned skills with strong empty-library and adaptive-memory controls. In one complete eight-batch development stream, continued evolution exceeded the first-batch frozen library by 9.39 valuation F1 points and 0.86 classification F1 points across all 112 later TRAIN sources, using first-encounter predictions rather than replay. ## 1. Introduction / 引言初稿

External skill libraries allow a frozen language model to reuse procedural knowledge across tasks. An evolving playbook changes what the model can consult while keeping its underlying weights fixed. ACE studies context adaptation through generation, reflection, and curation [2]. The question in this work is narrower: can experience accumulated over successive batches improve a small model's application of legal rules to previously unanswered cases, beyond the gains from a static legal prior or additional computational support?

Legal exemption reasoning offers a setting in which factual eligibility and numerical consequences can be assessed separately. OpenExempt generates problems from symbolic representations of federal and state exemption law and supplies machine-computable reference solutions [1]. We study two of its tasks. Exemption classification requires identifying applicable provisions for each asset. Exemption valuation additionally requires calculating the protectable amount under each applicable provision. These tasks permit error analysis of asset grounding, statutory selection, and numerical execution; they do not evaluate real-client consultation or the full practice of legal advice.

A convincing skill-learning result requires more than a larger library or an improved replay score. A static prior can outperform an empty prompt before any experience is collected, and changing the answer interface can affect performance independently of learning. A candidate can also improve a training average while damaging other legal contexts. We therefore distinguish first-encounter performance, post-update training replay, and final frozen-library testing. We inspect which edits are published and which guidance reaches each subsequent case under a shared answer interface.

Our research hypothesis is that controlling the scope of reusable edits can improve experience transfer while limiting interference between legal contexts. SkillCommit studies behaviorally validated scope expansion and replay-based preservation [3]. Here, we examine edit dependencies induced by publicly co-visible legal contexts while retaining the executor's direct legal answer interface. 外部技能库让参数冻结的语言模型能够跨任务复用程序性知识。技能库的进化改变模型可以利用的经验，而不更新其权重。ACE通过生成、反思和策展研究上下文适应[2]。本文关注更具体的问题：连续批次积累的经验，能否让小模型更好地将法律规则应用于此前未回答的案件，并产生超出静态法律先验和额外计算支持的收益？

法律资产豁免任务允许分别检验事实适用与数值后果。OpenExempt基于联邦及州豁免法律的符号表示生成问题，并提供可计算的参考答案[1]。本文研究其中的豁免分类和豁免估值：前者要求为每项资产识别全部适用法条，后者还要求计算每项适用豁免的可保护金额。因此可以分别分析资产事实对齐、法条选择和数值执行错误。这不是多轮客户咨询，也不等价于评估现实法律服务能力。

可信的技能学习结果不能只看技能库变大或训练重答得分提高。静态先验可能在学习开始前就胜过空库，回答接口改变也可能独立影响成绩；候选也可能在训练平均分上获益，却损害其他法律范围。因此本文区分首次遇案表现、更新后的训练重放和最终冻结库测试，并在共享回答接口下检查实际发布的编辑和后续案件收到的指导。

本文假设：约束可复用编辑的作用范围，有助于训练经验迁移并减少不同法律情境间的干扰。SkillCommit研究了经行为验证的作用域扩张和基于重放的行为保持[3]。本文检验公开共同可见的法律情境所诱导的编辑依赖，并保留执行器直接作答的法律接口。[待填分析：通过近邻方法比较和既定消融确定方法差异。][待填结果：整合正式对比的发现。]

## 1.1 Related work / 相关工作

**Experience-based memory.** External memory provides a learning channel for agents whose parameters remain fixed. Experience-based systems extract natural-language insights and recall previous trials, while workflow memories induce recurring action routines that can be supplied to later tasks [5,6]. Reasoning memories use both self-judged successes and failures, and memory-aware scaling adds contrastive experience for distillation [7]. These approaches differ in storage, feedback, and retrieval; our evaluation distinguishes a newly learned prior from continued revision through a fixed first-batch library, and retains an empty-library condition under the same legal answer interface.

**Procedural consolidation and legal evaluation.** Procedural consolidation turns trajectory evidence into reusable resources, with distinct criteria for deciding when an abstraction is useful. Global playbook adaptation uses generation, reflection, and curation [2]; parallel skill induction merges trajectory-local patches into portable files [4]; behaviorally validated consolidation uses source replay and cross-instance compatibility before broadening a skill's scope [3]. Complementary audits examine artifact–executor compatibility and regressions of previously correct behavior separately from aggregate utility [8], while OpenExempt supplies a statutory-rule environment with machine-computable reference solutions [1]. We assess proposed edits in dependency-closed groups determined by the public legal contexts rendered together, use seen-source replay to control publication, and distinguish that training operation from subsequent-case and frozen-library evaluations.

**基于经验的记忆。** 外部记忆为参数保持冻结的智能体提供学习渠道。经验式系统从历史任务中提取自然语言见解并回忆既往尝试，工作流记忆则归纳可供后续任务使用的重复行动程序[5,6]。推理记忆同时利用智能体自判成功与失败的经验，并通过记忆感知的扩展计算增加用于归纳的对比经验[7]。这些方法在存储、反馈和检索方式上有所不同；本文用固定首批学习库区分初次获得先验与持续修订的价值，并在相同法律回答接口下保留空库条件。

**程序性归纳与法律评价。** 程序性归纳将轨迹证据转为可复用资源，不同方法采用不同标准判断抽象何时有用。全局playbook适应采用生成、反思和策展[2]；并行技能归纳将轨迹局部补丁合并为可携带的技能文件[4]；经行为验证的归纳则在扩张技能范围之前，使用源案重放和跨实例兼容性检查[3]。补充性审计将技能产物与执行器的兼容性、既有正确行为的退化作为区别于总体效用的评价问题[8]，OpenExempt则提供具有可计算参考答案的法律规则环境[1]。本文依据共同呈现的公开法律情境，将提案编辑分为依赖闭合的组，以已见来源重放控制发布，并将这项训练操作与后续新案和冻结库评价分开。

## 2. Task and data / 任务与数据

**Task definition.** For each source case, the actor receives the task's public facts, statutes, and supplied solved steps. In the current configuration, allowable exemption jurisdictions are given; the actor is not asked to re-solve the preceding jurisdiction-selection task. EC returns a mapping from asset descriptions to applicable statutory citations. EV returns the corresponding citation–amount claims. EC and EV share an underlying source case, although their natural-language fact renderings need not be identical. They are correlated task views, not two independent datasets.

**Data provenance.** Development cases are generated with the unmodified OpenExempt generator, pinned at commit `ec6fa2547431096588ce2d3ef107b8adc33cd4fe` [1]. The current training stream contains 128 generated source cases, identifiers 181–308, divided into eight batches of 16, with both EC and EV tasks for each source. The dataset generation seed is 2026102101. Settings specify two to four assets, two to four domiciles, a married-case ratio of 0.5, and state jurisdictions Arizona, Illinois, Oregon, Pennsylvania, and Wisconsin; irrelevant facts and asset/domicile opinions are disabled. Federal provisions can also appear in the public allowable framework. These 128 generated sources remain training/development data. Earlier development runs exposed the first 48 sources and partial predictions for two later sources to the researcher; a first encounter here means that the current learner has not yet answered the source. The profile is a custom benchmark configuration, not the official Advanced Competency profile. A separate sealed IID test packet contains 128 source cases; its semantic contents, predictions, and outcomes have not been inspected.

**Information boundary.** All five logical control/treatment records for each task in a batch are recorded before that batch's targets are released for grading and learning. The teacher can access released training targets, but neither actor nor teacher receives private symbolic construction fields as an inference tool. The benchmark solver is used for data construction and target generation, not as an actor tool. No separate validation set is used: seen-source replay is part of training optimization. Previously inspected sources remain development/training data and cannot later be relabelled as held-out test data.

**任务定义。** 律师执行器接收任务公开事实、法律和题目给定的前序解答。当前配置已给出可选豁免法域，不要求模型重新求解前序法域识别任务。EC输出资产描述到全部适用法条的映射；EV进一步输出法条与金额。EC和EV来自同一底层来源案件，但自然语言事实表述不必逐字相同，不能算成两个独立数据集。

**数据来源。** 开发数据使用未修改的OpenExempt生成器，固定版本为上述提交[1]。当前训练流包含181–308共128来源、八批、每批16来源，每来源各有EC和EV任务；数据生成seed为2026102101。每案2至4项资产、2至4居所、已婚比例0.5，不加入无关事实及资产/居所意见；州范围覆盖亚利桑那、伊利诺伊、俄勒冈、宾夕法尼亚和威斯康星，给定框架中也可包含联邦豁免。这128来源始终是训练或开发数据。此前开发运行已向研究者暴露前48来源反馈及后续两个来源的部分预测；这里的首次遇案指当前学习器尚未回答该来源，不是研究者未见。该设置是自定义配置，不是官方Advanced Competency。另有独立封存的IID128来源测试packet，其语义内容、预测和成绩均未读取。

**信息边界。** 每批每任务五个逻辑条件的预测全部保存后，才释放本批标签用于评分和学习。教师可以读取已释放训练答案，但律师和教师不能将私有符号字段作为推理工具。基准求解器只用于数据构建与生成标签，不是律师工具。不另设验证集，已见来源重放属于训练优化。被反复审查过的来源始终保留训练或开发身份，不能改名为隔离测试。

## 3. Method / 方法

Our method alternates direct legal prediction with feedback-guided revision of a compact procedural library. Public legal dependencies determine which proposed edits are assessed and published together. The [method overview and bilingual caption](figures/method_overview_caption_20261023.md) show the implemented information flow; exact source and configuration snapshots are specified in [Appendix D](appendix_experimental_details_20261022.md).

本方法交替进行直接法律作答和由反馈驱动的紧凑程序性技能库修订，以公开法律依赖确定哪些编辑应共同评估与发布。方法图展示实际信息流，精确源码及配置版本见附录D。后续预测只使用实际已发布库。

### 3.1 Frozen actor and procedural skills / 冻结执行器与程序性技能

The actor is Qwen3-8B at model revision `b968826d9c46dd6066d109eabc6255188de91218`; its weights remain fixed throughout learning and inference. Skills contain EC/EV-specific procedural text and a public legal scope. Guidance is selected by the supplied task and allowable jurisdictions, rather than hidden target annotations or a learned answer router. A skill specifies factual conditions to inspect, the legal alternatives they enable, and exceptions that prevent unsupported claims. Numerical rules, when needed, are expressed in plain language.

The actor directly returns asset–citation sets for EC and citation–amount claims for EV. The same system instruction, public task, citation vocabulary, response schema, and generation budget apply to the empty-library control; the learned guidance is the intervention. Public asset and citation labels are decoded through their declared mappings; asset identifiers denote literal public mentions, not hidden ownership or eligibility. EC and EV are independently answered tasks: no predicted EC answer is passed into EV. No numerical interpreter, missing-claim insertion, asset-name repair, additional inference model, or answer-dependent retry is used. The current 4,096-token cap counts the complete serialized library, including metadata.

执行器为固定上述版本的Qwen3-8B，学习和推理中均不更新权重。技能包含EC/EV各自的程序性文本和公开法律范围，依据给定任务与可选法域投递，不依赖隐藏答案或学习得到的答案路由器。技能说明需要检查的事实条件、相应法律选项及排除不当主张的例外，必要的金额规则以自然语言表达。

执行器直接输出EC的资产法条集合及EV的法条金额主张。空库与带库共用系统指令、公开任务、法律词表、回答schema和生成预算，处理差别是学习得到的指导。公开资产和法条标签仅按已声明映射解码，资产标识来自题面字面提及，不编码隐藏的所有权或适用性。EC与EV独立作答，不将预测EC答案传给EV。不使用数值解释器、不补漏主张、不修资产名称、不增加推理模型或按答案重试。当前4,096-token上限计算完整序列化库及元数据。

### 3.2 Teacher updates and publication / 教师更新与发布

After all first-encounter predictions in a batch are recorded, the teacher receives released training targets, actual responses, scores, and library state. The current teacher is `gpt-5.6-sol` with high reasoning effort. It can propose create, update, merge, or delete operations, or explicitly make no change, with at most two candidate attempts per batch; an attempt is not limited to one skill edit. The teacher is instructed to repair observed factual conditions, statutory alternatives, exceptions, or amount reasoning while preserving correct unrelated boundaries. Its communication packet contains all 16 current sources and at most 16 past sources, each with both task views. Historical selection prioritizes a difficult witness from each public allowable-jurisdiction context, then observed proposal regressions and remaining parent failures, with deterministic source-ID tie-breaking. Admission nevertheless replays all seen training sources. The packet also contains the complete public statutes and bounded excerpts of actual model reasoning, at most the last 1,024 characters per included trace; those excerpts are not verified causal explanations.

For each task, public legal contexts define dependencies between edits: jurisdictions that occur together, moved skill identities, and changed shared guidance connect affected scopes. Connected components determine which edits are assessed together. Each accepted component needs current-source evidence and preserves native F1, complete-output F1, negative-or-unscored-asset claim counts, and invalid-format counts on current and prior sources for the affected task. Strict current improvement can be a higher native or complete-output F1, or fewer of these audited errors. Accepted components form one library that must fit the total cap and pass the corresponding whole-library retention gate for both EC and EV. Composed requests must match already observed parent or proposal requests exactly; their raw responses are decoded afresh rather than selecting answers by case outcome. These checks constrain training averages, not every case or future-case performance.

The feedback policy preserves accepted components while exposing unresolved proposal failures to the next permitted attempt. After a partial acceptance, the committed subset becomes the real parent for the next proposal; a later rejection does not undo it. Bounded failure witnesses, at most eight task records per proposal, can be carried from the immediately preceding batch publication, rather than an unbounded history. After refinement, the final bank must also pass the full retention gate against the original batch parent. Each batch writes one final publication, including an unchanged bank when no edit is admitted. This is supervised TRAIN optimization, not group-relative policy-gradient training or label-free trajectory learning.

本批全部首次预测完成后，教师获得已释放训练答案、实际回答、分数和当前库。当前教师为`gpt-5.6-sol`，high推理强度；每批至多两次候选尝试，支持创建、更新、合并、删除或明确不改，一次尝试可以编辑多条技能。教师按真实错误修正事实条件、法律选项、例外或金额推理，并保留正确的无关边界。通信包含全部16个本批来源及至多16个历史来源，每来源有两个任务视图；历史优先按公开法域情境取困难见证，再纳入真实提案退化及父库失败，按来源ID确定平局顺序。准入仍重放全部已见训练来源。另给完整公开法条和实际模型推理的有界尾部摘录，每条至多1,024字符，不把这些摘录当作已证实的因果解释。

每个任务分别从公开法律情境建立编辑依赖：共同出现的法域、技能标识的范围移动和共享指导改变连接受影响范围，由连通分量确定必须共同评估的编辑。通过分量须有本批来源证据，并在受影响任务的本批及历史来源保持原F1与完整输出F1、负目标或未计分资产声明数不增加、格式错误数不增加。严格本批改善可以是原F1或完整输出F1提高，或上述错误计数减少。通过分量组成一个库，再检查总容量及EC、EV两任务对应的整库保持门槛。组合请求必须精确匹配已观测父库或提案请求，原始回答重新解码，不按案件结果挑答案。检查约束训练均值，不保证每案或未来新案不下降。

反馈策略保留已通过分量，并将未解决的提案失败传给本批下一次允许的尝试。部分接收后的真实库成为下一提案的父库，后续拒收不会撤回它。来自紧邻上一批发布的失败见证可以有界传递，每份提案最多八个任务记录，不无限累积历史。细化后的末库还须相对本批原始父库再次通过完整保持门槛。每批只写一次最终发布，无修改通过时发布原库。这是标签辅助的TRAIN优化，不是组内相对策略梯度训练，也不是纯轨迹无标签学习。

### 3.3 Initialization, alternatives, and learning evidence / 初始化、替代方案与学习证据

The current run initializes an empty library once; subsequent batches use the actually published library. In addition to the empty-initial alias, a separate control freezes this run's unique final B1 publication before B2 starts and never updates it. This own-B1 library is evaluated but is not supplied to the teacher or admission procedure. It equals the online library during B2 by construction; B3 is the first batch that can distinguish continued updating from the initial learning episode. The implementation also exposes task-level and whole-library submission policies for separately frozen ablations. Historical program-output experiments motivate the shared direct answer interface but are not spliced into its learning curve. Scope/replay mechanisms have prior art [3]; code-level checks or retrospective combinations cannot establish either novelty or subsequent-case benefit.

本运行仅在初始化时空库，后续各批使用实际发布的库。除了代表空库的固定初始名称，另在B2开始前冻结同一次学习的唯一最终B1发布，并始终不再更新。自身B1库只评测，不送教师或准入。它在B2与在线库按设计相同；B3起才可检验继续更新相对初次学习的增益。实现也保留整任务与整库提交策略，用于另行冻结的消融。历史程序输出实验为改用共享直接回答接口提供开发动机，不与新曲线拼接。作用域与重放已有相关工作[3]，代码检查或回顾性组合均不能证明原创性或后续新案收益。

## 4. Evaluation / 评价设计

**Research questions.** RQ1 asks whether learned skills improve EC and EV on source-disjoint cases relative to an empty actor with the same per-answer inference settings, a blind static legal library, and two explicitly adapted experience methods. RQ2 asks whether continued updates improve on this learner's fixed B1 library, rather than only on an empty prompt. RQ3 asks how public-context component submission and bounded closed-loop refinement change the learned library, adverse effects, and cost. These questions correspond to the declared comparisons and two learning ablations; this study does not include cross-backbone or unseen-jurisdiction experiments. Matching per-answer generation limits does not match total learning computation or memory capacity.

**Shared execution.** The completed main run uses one A40 GPU and one frozen bf16 Qwen3-8B instance, served by vLLM 0.10.2. Context and output limits are 32,768 and 8,192 tokens. Thinking is enabled; temperature is 0.6, top-p 0.95, top-k 20, learning seed 45, and server seed 42. The server permits one concurrent sequence and disables prefix caching. Planned additional learning seeds are 145 and 245; final evaluation uses a common seed 45 for all frozen libraries, not three independent decoding replicates. Exact prompts, schemas, model revision, the shared EOS-alignment dependency patch, and environment hashes are retained with the run. The teacher can optimize on released TRAIN targets; there is no separate validation set and no test-feedback learning.

**Endpoints.** EC and EV use the pinned original OpenExempt evaluator. EC measures citation-set agreement after its asset matching. EV additionally requires a citation–amount match under the upstream numerical tolerance. The implementation first obtains native per-case scores and then averages them separately by task. These are not the evidence-extraction F1 scores of the project's earlier ContractNLI experiments. The upstream scorer can ignore unmatched extra predicted assets, and native EC scoring can assign zero to both correct empty predictions and false claims on negative-target assets. Separately labelled complete-output EC and EV endpoints count all predicted claims using the same task-specific claim-matching rules; negative-or-unscored-asset claims and invalid outputs are also reported. It is calculated within case and then averaged across cases, rather than pooled across all cases.

**Controls and estimands.** The development comparison is the evolving library versus the strong direct-answer empty library on the same first-encounter sources. The implementation retains three control names for audit continuity, but empty, fixed-initial, and direct-empty now produce identical requests; they are one reference condition rather than three independent baselines. Identical requests share a raw response and are not independent samples. B1 cannot establish online learning because the library is still empty. The own-B1 control supplies a separate, fixed learned library. The zero-case static legal library was compiled without trajectories or targets; its complete TRAIN control and the ACE seed-45 stream are now available, while final frozen-library comparisons remain pending.

**Mechanism and cost.** Each batch should report source counts; first-encounter scores; paired gains and harms; proposed, rejected, and published edits; parent/child hashes; library size and tokens; guidance actually delivered; invalid responses; physical model calls; teacher attempts and tokens; and wall-clock time. Text delivery is not proof that a skill caused an improvement; controlled memory interventions and policy ablations remain necessary for attribution. More skills, more calls, or more successful training gates do not themselves demonstrate useful learning.

The fixed static library completed its full-TRAIN inference control on 23 October. It uses the same 128 sources, direct actor, generation settings and public scope projection, without a new teacher or library update. All 256 predictions were saved before grading. Its descriptive comparisons use the actual evolving first forecasts and common empty reference, not a terminal main-bank replay or a new validation/test split. The static library starts complete, whereas the main library starts empty and changes using released TRAIN feedback; this contrast alone does not isolate the update mechanism. It adds no final TEST arm or primary contrast. Exact endpoint, information-access, cost and fairness definitions are in [Appendix D](appendix_experimental_details_20261022.md); the [execution protocol](../docs/research/OPENEXEMPT_FIXED_STATIC_TRAIN_CONTROL_V2_20261022.md) preserves the unchanged zero-case library and control algorithm.

The complementary Trace2Skill adaptation [4] contrasts offline trajectory-wise induction with ACE's online playbook. ### 5.2 Published skills and actual cost / 真实发布技能与成本

The published library changed through targeted revisions rather than mandatory growth. All 15 actual teacher responses, 14 non-NOOP candidate decisions, and eight publication hashes were reconstructed. Nine candidate attempts were accepted, five were rejected, and one response explicitly made no change. Five accepted attempts contained a complete proposal that failed the whole-bank gate, but an admitted composition of public-context components passed it. This establishes that component submission affected actual publications; it does not establish the counterfactual outcome of independently training the whole-bank ablation. Across the nine admitted compositions, all 1,152 repeated TRAIN task views reused exact-request raw responses and were decoded afresh, with zero additional model generations for composition.

Public-context submission also changed decisions within a task. Of the nine accepted compositions, four differed structurally from taking all edits approved by the EC/EV task-level gates. In the second B1 proposal, the full EV update failed retention, but its Illinois component was retained. On all three sources in that component, mean EV F1 rose from 57.41% to 79.63% against the actual first-candidate parent. This fixed-proposal analysis locates a finer-grained TRAIN benefit; it is not an independently learned task-local ablation or evidence of future-case superiority.

Teacher proposals contained nine CREATE and 24 UPDATE operations. Actual committed structural mutations were seven creations and 14 updates across the run, counting repeated revisions rather than distinct skills. There were no observed deletions or merges. The final library contains seven records and 2,486 serialized tokens: jurisdiction-conditioned guidance about category boundaries, independent general-property alternatives, amount caps, and joint ownership. ### 5.3 Where continued guidance changed / 持续指导实际改变的位置

The modest continued EC gain was accompanied by limited change in delivered EC guidance, rather than an observed failure to deliver an entirely new library. Across all 96 B3–B8 sources, 80 EC requests had the same visible guidance, complete HTTP request, native response origin, decoded answer, and score as own-B1. The remaining 16 EC tasks had ten improvements, three harms, and three ties. EV guidance changed for 75 of the same 96 sources, with 45 improvements, eight harms, and 22 ties; the other 21 requests were unchanged. The full 96-source mean differences remain +1.00 EC and +10.96 EV points. The changed subsets are an observational exposure decomposition, not alternative primary denominators or a randomized test of skill causality.

Admission records also show a substantive trade-off rather than eleven interchangeable opportunities for improvement. Eight post-B1 proposals edited 11 EC scope components: two were admitted, five were rejected with a native-F1 loss, and four were rejected with a complete-output or negative-asset-claim loss despite no native-F1 loss. These records do not establish that simply relaxing admission would improve new-source performance; the independently learned ablations remain pending.

## 6. Discussion and scope / 讨论与边界

The development stream suggests that procedural memory can support continued improvement in legal valuation beyond the initial learned prior. Its larger EV than EC gain points to a useful distinction: retaining an applicable citation and correctly computing its consequence are related but separable learning outcomes. The proposed dependency-aware submission mechanism makes the unit of skill revision explicit. Whether that mechanism explains the observed benefit, and whether it transfers to source-disjoint cases, are the questions for the independently learned ablations and frozen-library test.

开发轨迹提示：程序性记忆可以在初次学得的先验之上，继续改善法律金额适用。EV收益大于EC，说明保留适用法条与正确计算法律后果是相关但可分辨的学习结果。所提出的依赖感知提交机制明确了技能修订的评估单位；机制能否解释实际收益、能否迁移到隔离新案，仍由独立学习消融和冻结库测试检验。

The present setup studies synthetic statutory-rule application in the exemption domain with supplied laws and allowable jurisdictions. It does not establish competence on Chinese law, open-world legal retrieval, real-client advice, or broad legal reasoning. Replay-based preservation is a training property and need not generalize to unseen cases. Known TRAIN ambiguities involving joint ownership and personal-versus-business use have not been adjudicated by legal experts; original cases, targets, and scores remain unchanged. Benchmark agreement is therefore not expert-validated legal correctness. The final discussion must examine negative transfer, teacher supervision and cost, template dependence, benchmark-scoring limitations, and whether guidance changes legal selection rather than merely response presentation. [PENDING RESULT — conclusions warranted by the completed formal study.]

当前范围是给定法律与可选法域下、豁免领域的合成法律适用，不证明中国法、开放法律检索、真实咨询或广泛法律推理能力。重放保持是训练性质，不保证新案。已知TRAIN中共同所有权及个人/商业用途的标注疑点尚未由法律专家裁定，原案例、标签和分数均保持，不把benchmark一致性当作专家验证的法律正确性。最终讨论必须解释负迁移、教师监督与成本、模板依赖、评分局限，以及指导是否改变法律选择而非仅改变呈现方式。[待正式结果形成结论。]
